# UNTITLED CITY EXTRACTION

## PvE Extraction Survival In A Living Urban Collapse

> *The streets are death.
> The buildings are temporary mercy.
> And the city only gets worse the longer you stay.*

---

# About The Game

**Untitled City Extraction** is a co-op PvE extraction shooter set inside a massive quarantined city slowly collapsing under escalating biological outbreaks, environmental shifts, and overwhelming hostile pressure.

Inspired by the tension of Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Extraction, the freedom of open urban survival games, and the risk-driven structure of extraction shooters, the game transforms the traditional extraction formula into something more dynamic and oppressive.

The city is not a collection of isolated missions.

It is one connected, hostile environment.

Players move freely through streets, alleyways, underground systems, rooftops, parking structures, apartment blocks, offices, hospitals, shopping centers, and quarantined sectors while choosing when to push deeper, when to hold inside a building, and when to extract before the city becomes impossible to survive.

Unlike traditional extraction shooters where interiors are usually the dangerous part, this game reverses the structure:

* The streets are exposure.
* Buildings are temporary safety.
* And every phase makes the outside more hostile.

You are not fighting to clear a map.

You are fighting to survive a city that continuously evolves around you.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Deploy into a live urban district**
2. **Scavenge streets and gather supplies**
3. **Enter buildings to complete objectives**
4. **Use interiors as temporary safe spaces**
5. **Survive escalating city phases**
6. **Decide whether to push deeper or extract**
7. **Reach extraction before the city overwhelms your squad**
8. **Return with resources, recovered gear, and survivors**

The longer you remain in the city, the more unstable and dangerous the environment becomes.

A successful squad constantly balances:

* greed
* survival
* exploration
* risk
* and timing

Every additional building cleared increases potential rewards.
But every minute spent inside the city gives the outbreak more time to spread.

---

# The City

The city itself is the main character.

It is not a static map.
It is a shifting combat ecosystem.

Entire districts can:

* become overrun
* lose power
* flood
* burn
* collapse into chaos
* or temporarily become survivable again

Players are free to roam the city streets at any time, but remaining outside for too long becomes increasingly dangerous as phases escalate.

The city is designed around contrast:

## Outside

The streets are:

* open
* loud
* exposed
* unpredictable

Players can be attacked from:

* rooftops
* windows
* alleyways
* underground tunnels
* collapsing infrastructure

Large enemy groups roam dynamically through districts.
Open areas become progressively harder to survive during later phases.

The streets create pressure.

---

## Inside

Buildings are safer — but never fully safe.

Entering a building creates temporary control:

* fewer attack angles
* slower pacing
* opportunities to regroup
* tactical planning
* resource management

But interiors also create new dangers:

* close quarters combat
* limited visibility
* trapped routes
* ambushes
* infestations
* breaching enemies

Players constantly transition between:

* outdoor panic
* indoor control
* indoor pressure
* outdoor desperation

This rhythm becomes the emotional core of the game.

---

# Dynamic Phase System

The city evolves during every run.

There is no traditional battle royale circle.
No artificial shrinking zone.

Instead, the world itself becomes more dangerous over time.

## Phase 1 — Initial Collapse

* Streets remain manageable
* Enemy density is lower
* Buildings are relatively secure
* Squads can move freely

This phase rewards exploration and planning.

---

## Phase 2 — Escalation

* Roaming enemy groups increase
* Special infected appear
* Streets become less predictable
* Noise begins attracting large swarms

Buildings become valuable strategic positions.

---

## Phase 3 — Overrun

The city begins actively consuming itself.

Examples:

* streets flood with infected
* power failures spread
* visibility drops
* elite enemies emerge
* buildings become vulnerable to breaches
* extraction points shut down or relocate

Players who stay too long begin fighting the city itself.

---

## Phase 4 — Critical State

Survival becomes nearly impossible.

The city is no longer meant to be controlled.
Only escaped.

Extraction becomes chaotic.
Safe routes disappear.
Buildings stop being reliable.

Players remaining during this phase are gambling everything for maximum rewards.

---

# Buildings & Zones

Buildings are not simple mission rooms.

They are survival tools.

Each structure serves as:

* objective space
* temporary shelter
* defensive stronghold
* loot opportunity
* extraction route
* or death trap

Different building types create completely different gameplay.

---

## Apartment Buildings

* narrow hallways
* vertical combat
* temporary safe rooms
* civilian loot
* multiple hidden routes

Strong for defensive play.

---

## Office Towers

* open interiors
* long sightlines
* dangerous glass exposure
* rooftop access
* high-value technological loot

Strong for scouting and risk-heavy objectives.

---

## Hospitals

* maze-like layouts
* medical resources
* emergency generators
* infected-heavy environments

High reward but highly dangerous.

---

## Shopping Centers

* large open interiors
* multiple entrances
* civilian supply caches
* strong defensive potential

Can temporarily become squad fortresses.

---

## Underground Systems

* subway tunnels
* maintenance corridors
* emergency shelters

Useful for movement during late phases but extremely claustrophobic.

---

# Objectives

Objectives are integrated naturally into the world.

Players may:

* recover research
* activate emergency systems
* rescue trapped civilians
* secure medical supplies
* restore communications
* locate missing squads
* gather outbreak samples
* reactivate extraction infrastructure

Objectives are not isolated levels.

Players must physically travel through the city to reach them.

---

# Flexible Specialist System

The game uses a flexible specialist/loadout system inspired by operator-based shooters, but without locking players into rigid hero characters.

Players build their role through:

* equipment
* abilities
* weapons
* gadgets
* support tools

The focus is adaptability, not hero identity.

---

# Shared Weapon System

Weapons are shared across all specialists.

Players are not restricted to:

* class-exclusive guns
* character-exclusive weapons
* rigid operator kits

Instead:

* abilities define playstyle
* weapons define combat preference

This allows experimentation without sacrificing identity.

# Enemy Design

Enemies are designed around behavior and pressure rather than pure damage scaling.

Different enemies force different reactions.

---

## Roamers

Basic infected that move through streets dynamically.

Dangerous in groups.

---

## Breachers

Enemies capable of:

* breaking barricades
* forcing entry
* destroying safe rooms

Prevent camping.

---

## Hunters

Fast enemies that track:

* sound
* movement
* extraction signals

Punish careless squads outdoors.

---

## Burrowers

Enemies emerging from:

* sewers
* walls
* underground systems

Create sudden interior threats.

---

## Titans

Massive late-phase enemies that turn districts into disaster zones.

Often avoided rather than fought directly.

---

# Extraction System

Extraction is a tactical decision, not a guaranteed ending.

Players may:

* extract early
* continue deeper
* move between districts
* recover teammates before leaving

Extraction points evolve dynamically based on:

* phase state
* weather
* power systems
* city control
* player actions

---

# Extraction Types

## Rooftop Helicopter Extraction

Fast but extremely loud.

Can shut down during storms or heavy infestation phases.

---

## Ground Vehicle Extraction

Safer for groups carrying heavy loot.

Requires clearing streets first.

---

## Underground Rail Extraction

Quiet but slow.

Can become trapped if tunnels collapse.

---

## Emergency Solo Extraction

Allows a single player to escape quickly with limited gear.

Useful during squad disasters.

---

# Risk & Reward System

The game constantly tempts players to stay longer.

Every additional objective:

* increases rewards
* improves loot quality
* unlocks deeper sectors

But also:

* escalates enemy pressure
* increases extraction difficulty
* destabilizes safe zones
* intensifies outdoor danger

The best rewards are found during the most dangerous phases.

---

# Replayability Without Live Service

The game is intentionally designed without relying on constant live-service updates.

Replayability comes from:

* dynamic phase systems
* evolving city conditions
* player-driven decisions
* flexible builds
* emergent squad stories
* unpredictable extractions
* changing environmental conditions

No two runs are meant to feel identical.

---

# Emergent Storytelling

The game focuses heavily on unscripted player stories.

Examples:

* a squad trapped inside a hospital during a city-wide blackout
* a failed extraction forcing players underground
* a teammate risking everything to recover another player's body
* a final rooftop defense while waiting for extraction during phase 4
* abandoning valuable loot just to survive

The game creates stories through systems — not cutscenes.

---

# Tone & Atmosphere

The atmosphere focuses on:

* urban isolation
* mounting pressure
* temporary safety
* environmental collapse
* desperation
* tactical survival

The city never truly feels conquered.

Only survived.

Every run feels like entering a living disaster zone that is slowly winning.

This is not a power fantasy.

This is not a hero shooter.

This is a game about:

* pressure
* adaptation
* tactical survival
* and knowing when to leave before the city takes everything from you.
